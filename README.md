# Insurance Agent Commission & Chargeback Reconciliation

Calculate producer commissions and chargebacks across policy lifecycle events, hierarchies, advances, and carrier schedules.

**Primary buyer:** Insurance carriers, MGAs, and agencies. **Evidence:** producer contracts, licenses, appointments, policies, premium, commission schedules, advances, cancellations, chargebacks, statements, and payments.

Full local application built with React, Vite, Express, PostgreSQL, and OpenRouter. Includes 15 domain-specific capabilities, 105 custom AI workbench fields, three scenario-fill controls per feature, operational registers, workflow transitions, analytics, professional AI decision briefs, audit history, and at least 15 PostgreSQL records per capability.

## Domain capabilities

- Producer contract library
- License appointment validation
- Policy premium ingestion
- Commission schedule mapping
- Hierarchy split calculation
- New business commission
- Renewal commission
- Advance commission tracking
- Cancellation chargeback
- Free-look reversal
- Replacement policy control
- Statement reconciliation
- Producer dispute workflow
- Cash payment reconciliation
- Carrier producer analytics

Run `./start.sh`, then open <http://127.0.0.1:4678>. API: `5678`.
